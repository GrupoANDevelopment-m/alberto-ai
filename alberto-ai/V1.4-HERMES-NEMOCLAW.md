### 18 Protected Path Patterns

Bloqueia acesso a:
- `/etc/shadow`, `/etc/passwd`, `/etc/sudoers`, `/etc/ssh/*`
- `~/.ssh/id_*` (RSA, ed25519, ecdsa)
- `~/.aws/credentials`, `~/.aws/config`
- `~/.kube/config`
- `~/.docker/config.json`
- `~/.npmrc`, `~/.pypirc`, `~/.netrc`
- `~/.git-credentials`, `~/.gitconfig`
- `/proc/*/environ`, `/proc/*/cmdline` (env dump via process)
- `/sys/*`, `/var/log/auth.log`, `/var/log/secure`
